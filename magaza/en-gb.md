## Description (≤4000)

Know exactly what you'll pay, and when. CashFollow turns your card purchases, instalments and Pay in 4 plans into one clear payment calendar.

Add what you bought and how you're paying. CashFollow uses each card's statement date and due date to work out when every payment lands, then shows your month-by-month plan and what's left.

■ All your payments in one plan
- Instalments: pick the number of payments; see the monthly amount and how many are left
- Pay in 4 (Klarna, Clearpay, PayPal): every payment every two weeks, and what you still owe
- This month at a glance: how much is already paid and what's left
- Revolving balances: add your card's APR to see interest, minimum payment and payoff time
- Statement dates: purchases up to the statement date go on this statement, the rest on the next
- Available credit: your limit minus what's still to be paid

■ Reminds you in time
- A heads-up before each due date
- The day before a statement closes: when today's purchases are due, and when later ones are
- When a payment plan is paid off: see how much it frees up each month
- A recap of last month at the start of every month

■ "Can I afford it?"
Before you buy, enter the price. CashFollow shows how each month's payments change, how much free cash you'll have left, and which card pushes the first payment furthest out.

■ Quick to add
- Past merchants in one tap: the card, number of payments and category fill in themselves
- "Again" adds the same purchase with today's date
- Subscriptions and rent: enter once, repeat every month

■ See your cash flow
- Payments by month, by card and by date
- Add your salary to see your free cash each month
- Heads-ups for payment spikes, nearly maxed-out cards and upcoming due dates
- Track cash, foreign currencies and gold
- Money you borrowed or lent, and loan payments

■ For the whole family
Track a partner's or family member's purchases in the same app, including who owes you what.

■ Private by design
No bank login, ever. CashFollow doesn't connect to your accounts, and you can start without creating an account. Your data is stored in your account only, never used for ads and never sold.

■ AI assistant
Connect CashFollow to an AI assistant such as ChatGPT or Claude and just ask: "Add the TV in 4 payments on my Visa", "How much do I pay next month?"

■ iPhone and web, same data
Use the same account at cashfollow.app.

CashFollow Premium (subscription) unlocks "Can I afford it?", the payment plan for the months ahead, heads-ups, the AI assistant connection and more. Prices and terms are shown in the app. Payment is charged to your Apple ID; the subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period.
