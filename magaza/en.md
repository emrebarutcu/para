## Description (≤4000)

Know exactly what you'll pay, and when. CashFollow turns your card purchases into a clear payment calendar.

Add what you bought and how you're paying. CashFollow uses each card's statement closing date and due date to work out when every payment lands, then shows your month-by-month plan and what's left.

■ All your payments in one plan
- Installments: pick the number of payments; see the monthly amount and how many are left
- Pay in 4 (Klarna, Afterpay, Affirm, PayPal, Zip, Sezzle): every payment every two weeks, and what you still owe
- Revolving balances: add your card's APR to see interest, minimum payment and payoff time
- Statement dates: purchases up to the closing date go on this statement, the rest on the next
- Available credit: your limit minus what's still to be paid

■ "Can I afford it?"
Before you buy, enter the price. CashFollow shows how each month's payments change, how much free cash you'll have left, and which card pushes the first payment furthest out.

■ See your cash flow
- Payments by month, by card and by date
- Add your paycheck to see your free cash each month
- Heads-ups for payment spikes, nearly maxed-out cards and upcoming due dates
- Track cash, foreign currencies and gold
- Money you borrowed or lent, and loan payments

■ For the whole family
Track a partner's or family member's purchases in the same app, including who owes you what.

■ Private by design
No bank login, ever. CashFollow doesn't connect to your accounts. Your data is stored in your account only, never used for ads and never sold.

■ AI assistant
Connect CashFollow to an AI assistant such as ChatGPT or Claude and just ask: "Add the TV in 4 payments on my Visa", "How much do I pay next month?"

■ iPhone and web, same data
Use the same account at cashfollow.app.

CashFollow Premium (subscription) unlocks "Can I afford it?", the AI assistant connection and more. Prices and terms are shown in the app. Payment is charged to your Apple ID; the subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period.
