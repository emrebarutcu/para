Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

CashFollow helps you plan credit card payments, installments, cash flow and net worth. This policy explains what data the CashFollow app (iPhone) and website (cashfollow.app) handle, why, and the choices you have. The short version: your financial records are yours, we use them only to run the service for you, we do not sell them and we do not show ads.

What you can use without an account

You can use CashFollow without creating an account. Your records then stay only on your device (in the app's storage or your browser's local storage) and are not sent to us, except for the requests described under “Market data” and “Category suggestions” below.

Data we store when you create an account

  • Account data: your email address, a password (stored by our authentication provider as a salted hash, never in plain text), your country (which sets your currency) and your time zone (so “today” is your day).
  • Your records: what you enter — people, cards (name, limit, statement and due days, optionally the last 4 digits), purchases and installments, income and asset entries, debts, loans and payments. We never ask for or store full card numbers, bank logins or account numbers.
  • Forwarded bank emails (optional, Turkey only): if you set up a personal forwarding address, the emails you forward to it are stored so purchases can be suggested from them. You can dismiss them at any time, and they are deleted with your account.

We use this data only to provide the service: to sync your records between your devices, to compute payment dates, cash flow and insights, and to let AI assistants you connect read and update your records on your instruction.

Usage analytics

To understand which features are used and to improve the app, we collect product usage events (for example “purchase added” or “screen opened”), together with a random identifier, app version, device type and approximate country. These events do not include the amounts, names or descriptions you enter. We do not use them for advertising and do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. We may use them to test variations of the app (for example, two versions of a screen).

Service providers

We rely on these providers to run CashFollow. They process data on our behalf and only as needed to provide their service:

  • Supabase — account authentication and database for synced records.
  • Cloudflare — website hosting, server functions, email forwarding addresses, and the AI model that suggests a category from a purchase description (only the description text is sent).
  • Product analytics provider — usage events as described above.
  • Apple — App Store purchases and subscriptions. We receive the status of your subscription, not your payment details.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr — the website loads a font and a library from these services, which receive your IP address as part of a normal web request.

Market data

To show exchange rates and fund or stock prices, the app requests them from our server, which fetches them from public sources. These requests contain only the currency or ticker codes, not your records.

AI assistants you connect

If you connect an AI assistant (such as Claude or ChatGPT) to your account, you authorize it to read and change your records through CashFollow. What you share with the assistant is also subject to that provider's privacy policy. You can disconnect it at any time in the assistant's settings.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal data, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not connect to your bank accounts.

Retention and deletion

We keep your account data and records for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time — in the iPhone app under Settings → Delete account, or on the website in the account window. This permanently deletes your account and all of your records from our database. Backups that may still contain them are overwritten within 30 days. Data stored only on your device is deleted when you delete the app or clear the browser's storage.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Each account can only read its own records (row-level security in the database). On iPhone, your sign-in session is kept in the Keychain.

Your rights

You can access and export your records (website: Settings → Export), correct them in the app, and delete them with your account. Depending on where you live — for example California (CCPA/CPRA), the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom (GDPR), or Turkey (KVKK) — you may have additional rights, such as to know what we hold about you or to object to processing. To exercise them, contact us at the address below. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Children

CashFollow is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.
