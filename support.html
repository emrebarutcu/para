Do I need an account?

No. Without an account your records stay only on your device. Create an account (Sign in in the app or on cashfollow.app) to back them up and to see the same records on your iPhone and on the website.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password and follow the link in the email we send you.

Does CashFollow connect to my bank?

No. CashFollow never asks for bank logins or card numbers; you enter your cards, purchases and payments yourself.

Why is my interest or minimum payment different from my bank's?

For cards with an APR, CashFollow estimates interest monthly (APR ÷ 12 on the balance carried past the due date) and the minimum payment with a common US rule. Banks use daily balances and their own rules, so their numbers can differ slightly; your statement is always the reference.

How do I change the app's language?

On iPhone, open Settings → CashFollow → Language. The app is available in English and Turkish.

How do I export or delete my data?

On the website, Settings → Export downloads all your records. To delete your account and all of its records, use Settings → Delete account in the iPhone app, or Delete account in the account window on the website. This cannot be undone.
